The cell's most abundant species takes the slope given up, so face values still sum to one and stay in [0, 1], and the face gamma, e0 and density follow that composition at the cell's p and T, so a contact at uniform p and T stays uniform. Larger cuts still limit every scalar of the cell, since cutting one species alone where it vanishes over a cell (the fuel at a flame's burnout) is a differential diffusion that slowed a thickened flame by 6% of its flame speed.
