- With MUSCL, a mass fraction about to leave [0, 1] at a face by less than 1e-4 now limits only its own slope instead of the limiter value of every scalar of the cell: a trace species next to a cell without it (CO at the frozen-chemistry boundary, the fuel's tail in burnt gas, round-off such as 1e-34 against 0) put the cell's mass fractions, gamma and e0 at first order, on `flame_3d` two thirds of the cells, and made results follow the chemistry's noise (0.86 K with `explicit_step`, 0.55 K between `rtol` 1e-7 and 1e-8; now 1e-5 and 2e-7 K). The cell's most abundant species takes the slope given up, so face values still sum to one and stay in [0, 1], and the face gamma, e0 and density follow that composition at the cell's p and T, so a contact at uniform p and T stays uniform. Larger cuts still limit every scalar of the cell, since cutting one species alone where it vanishes over a cell (the fuel at a flame's burnout) is a differential diffusion that slowed a thickened flame by 6% of its flame speed. With TENO, a species within round-off of its cell value over the stencil no longer sets the bound-preserving factor to zero (#1169).
