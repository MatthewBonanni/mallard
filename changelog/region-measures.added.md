- Filled region probes record the region's own volume and surface area (2D: area and perimeter; axisymmetric: per radian), in closed form for a primitive and otherwise to a stated tolerance, and those of its part in the domain, whose surface is the region's own inside the domain plus the domain's boundary inside it. They are in the probe sampling block, which now also heads the reductions file of a probe without a samples file, in a `probe` record of `mallard.jsonl`, and in `mallard check`. A region more than 0.1% outside the domain gets a warning (`W-PROBE-OUTSIDE`) and records the share outside as `clipped`.
