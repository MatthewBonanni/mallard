- Lagrangian particles documentation: a rewritten [user guide](docs/guide/particles.md) (choosing tracers or solid particles, drag laws, the stiff limit, walls, pitfalls, accuracy and performance), a new theory chapter, references and figures generated by `tools/docs/particles.py` (#1226, #1367, #1376).
- Three particle examples, each with a script checking it against an exact solution: `particle_settling` (terminal settling, Schiller-Naumann and the stiff limit), `particle_bounce` (wall restitution under gravity) and `particle_centrifuge` (inertial spirals in solid-body rotation, deposition on the outer wall).
- Particle verification studies in the verification manual: the temporal order of every integrator for tracers and drag relaxation, also near the stiff limit; Stokes settling and the Schiller-Naumann step response; first-order convergence of the stiff limit as tau vanishes and of wall bounces in the step; and the spatial order of the gas velocity at the particles and of tracer pathlines on quads, triangles, hexahedra and tetrahedra for every scheme.
- The feature matrix has a row for Lagrangian particles, verified with each reconstruction and time integrator by the particle studies.
