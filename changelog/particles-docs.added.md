- Lagrangian particles documentation: a rewritten [user guide](docs/guide/particles.md) (choosing tracers or solid particles, the drag laws with a table of where Stokes, Schiller-Naumann and Singh et al. are valid and how to choose, stiff particles, walls, pitfalls, accuracy and performance), a new theory chapter, references and figures generated by `tools/docs/particles.py`, among them the drag coefficient and terminal velocities of all three drag laws (#1226, #1367, #1376, #1382).
- Three particle examples, each with a script checking it against an exact solution: `particle_settling` (terminal settling, Schiller-Naumann and the exponential integration of tiny particles), `particle_bounce` (wall restitution under gravity, bounces located within the step) and `particle_centrifuge` (inertial spirals in solid-body rotation, deposition on the outer wall).
- Particle verification studies in the verification manual: the temporal order of every integrator for tracers and drag relaxation, also near the switch to exponential integration; Stokes settling and the Schiller-Naumann step response; the convergence of the exponential integration of relaxations faster than the step; and the spatial order of the gas velocity at the particles and of tracer pathlines on quads, triangles, hexahedra and tetrahedra for every scheme.
- The feature matrix has a row for Lagrangian particles, verified with each reconstruction and time integrator by the particle studies.
