- `mallard restart convert SOURCE -i INPUT [-o FILE]` converts a restart file to the gas of another case on the same mesh: finite-rate chemistry of one mechanism to another (subset, superset or neither), and finite-rate chemistry to a flamelet table and back; `[initialize]` `type = "restart"` and `--restart` convert a restart of another gas the same way. Pressure, temperature and velocity are kept, species match by name (aliases for other names, `--alias "CH2*=CH2(S)"`), the mass of species the target lacks moves onto its species by an element-conserving projection (`element-projection`: elements exact, `Y >= 0`, closest to the source in the chi-square distance), `Z` and `C` come from the composition with the table's definitions, and density and energy from the target's thermodynamics. A conversion that is not exact prints its summary and writes nothing until the user accepts the rule (on a terminal, with `--accept-mapping element-projection`, or `[initialize.convert] accept`); `--dry-run` prints the summary only and `--format json` prints it for programs (schema `mallard-restart-convert`) ([#631](https://github.com/MatthewBonanni/mallard/issues/631), [#287](https://github.com/MatthewBonanni/mallard/issues/287)).
- The table format is version 2: `fpv` tables record how `Z` is defined (`/model_data/mixture_fraction = "bilger"`), and Mallard checks that the progress variable weighs only species of the species block, that `C = sum_k w_k Y_k` at every node and that the streams' mass fractions sum to 1. Tables of version 1 still run; converting a solution to or from one takes the definitions from `--progress-variable`, `--fuel` and `--oxidizer` or `[initialize.convert]`.
