- Bound-preserving TENO (`bound_preserving = true`) keeps density and pressure positive in every cell at `cfl` up to 0.5 in planar single-gas runs: the convective fluxes are blended toward first order on the faces where the update would leave a cell without positive density or pressure. Its face values alone did not guarantee this, and the Mach 8 `mallard` example on its cubic mesh diverged in the near vacuum behind the tail. Faces that need no limiting are unchanged.
