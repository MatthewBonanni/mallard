- `[[probes]]` `shape` is `type`, as in every region; `shape` is read with a warning until 0.10.0 (#499).
- Region types name one dimension each: `circle` and `ellipse` are curves, and `disk`, `rectangle` and `polygon` are flat (areas in 2D, surfaces in 3D). In `[[sources]]` regions, `type = "circle"` (a filled circle) is `"disk"` in 2D and `"sphere"` in 3D, `"ellipse"` is `"ellipsoid"`, and in 3D `"disk"` is `"sphere"` and `"rectangle"` `"box"`; the old names are read with a warning that names the new one until 0.10.0, and a region given them directly is an error that does (#499).
