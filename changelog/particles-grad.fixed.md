- The pressure-gradient, added-mass and Saffman lift forces on solid particles read the gas gradients at the particle from the polynomial the particle reads the gas from, differentiated where `point_values` evaluates it, instead of the cell's least-squares gradient: with TENO of order `k` they converge at order `k - 1` instead of first order. Runs without these forces are unchanged bit for bit, and viscous runs with particles no longer recompute the stage's least-squares gradients in the cells holding particles.
