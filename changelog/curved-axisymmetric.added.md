- Curved boundaries in axisymmetric runs: a sphere or nozzle contour in the meridian plane, from high-order Gmsh lines or `[[mesh.curved]]`, no longer has to be run on straight faces. The cells and faces next to it get their volumes, `r`-weighted centroids, face areas and TENO moments per radian from the curve, the curved faces' flux points are weighted by the radius so that uniform flow and gas at rest stay exact to round-off, TENO integrates the pressure source over the curved cells, and initial averages, region fractions and probe surfaces follow the curved geometry. TENO keeps its design order between curved walls, axis cells included (#725).
