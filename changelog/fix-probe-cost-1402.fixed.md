- Probes that write only reductions (`samples = false`, regions without averages, spectra or motion) are reduced on the GPU in the step graph and only their reductions are copied to the host. Before, each step they were written at copied every sample's values into pinned host memory and reduced them on the CPU, which made the perf set's `probes_3d` case (1.5M cut-plane points) take 170 to 400 ms longer per step than `teno5_3d` on an A100 (#1402). The written reductions are unchanged bit for bit.
