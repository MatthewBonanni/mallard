- Probes that write only reductions (`samples = false`, regions without averages, spectra or motion) are reduced on the GPU in the step graph, and only their reductions are copied to the host. Before, every step they were written at copied each sample's values into pinned host memory and reduced them on the CPU. With TENO, a probe's point values now fit each cell's polynomial once for all of its points instead of once per point. Together these remove most of the extra cost of the perf set's `probes_3d` case over `teno5_3d` on an A100 (#1402). Written values are unchanged bit for bit.
