- `mallard post render FILE... [--field NAME] [-o OUT]` draws images and movies of snapshots with no display, ParaView or Python: 2D cells, or 3D cells cut by `--slice`, sampled by area so that fine meshes do not alias, with a colormap chosen by the field, the time, a colorbar with units and a scale bar, light or dark (`--theme`), the cells' edges (`--mesh`) and probe files' outlines (`--probes`). It writes PNG, numbered PNG frames and animated GIF itself, and MP4, MOV, MKV or WebM through an `ffmpeg` on `PATH` ([Images and movies](https://matthewbonanni.github.io/mallard/docs/guide/output/#images-and-movies-mallard-post-render), #516).
