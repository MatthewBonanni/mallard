- The run's setup banner is in tables: each section's settings, then its quantities with their units in a column (cells, faces, the time step or CFL number, the stops, output and probe intervals, the hybrid flux's threshold, chemistry tolerances), the mesh's extent, cell sizes and owned cells per rank as min and max, the partition's ranks, halo layers and exchange sizes as rows, the boundaries as zone, condition, faces and details, and the outputs as file, interval with its units and contents. The models' constants are quantities with their units too: the gas's gamma, R, cp and cv, the viscosity law's, the source terms' (gravity, mass flow, region areas or volumes, sponges, radiation), TENO's stencil sizes and switch, the MUSCL limiter's, the step controller's tolerances and PID coefficients, and LES's, TFLES's and PaSR's. `TENO setup` does not repeat the Setup section's numerics time, and durations below a millisecond use the micro sign in a UTF-8 locale (#659, #675, #826, #849).
