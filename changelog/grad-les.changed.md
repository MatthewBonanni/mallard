- `viscous_gradients = "reconstruction"` now combines with `[les]`: the SGS models take the same TENO polynomial gradients as the viscous fluxes (`les.gradients` follows, and `"least_squares"` is then an error), from the wall fits in cells whose stencils mirror across walls, in each stage's reconstruction as in the state's. The Smagorinsky eddy viscosity of a wall-bounded TENO5 flow converges at order 3.7 to 3.8 in the wall cells instead of 1.4. With `les.gradients = "reconstruction"`, the global dynamic procedure and the thickened flame's vorticity now read the models' gradients instead of least squares.
