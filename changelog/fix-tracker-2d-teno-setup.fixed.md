- **2D TENO setup on GPUs**: batches of at most 16,384 cells, so the setup's workspace no longer grows with the mesh (up to 4-6 GB on an A100 at a million cells, above the run's own peak and the `check --memory` prediction); its tables take about 0.3 s (order 3) to 0.9 s (order 5) longer at a million cells. The setup also no longer makes unlabeled device allocations on uncurved meshes.
