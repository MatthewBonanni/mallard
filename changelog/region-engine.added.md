- Regions: the shared syntax for parts of space (spheres, cylinders, cones, boxes, tori, half-spaces, polygons, implicit `f(x, y, z) < 0`, their unions, intersections and differences, with `scale`, `rotate`, `translate` and an optional smooth `edge`), with exact cell volume fractions, in [docs/input.md](docs/input.md#regions); the keys that take a region follow in later releases (#317, #359).
