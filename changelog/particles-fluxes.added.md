- `[[particles.fluxes]]`: the particles crossing a surface or leaving through a boundary zone, counted per `interval` steps: parcels out, in and net, and for solid particles the physical particles, the mass and the mass rate, one CSV row per interval, bitwise the same on any number of ranks and without writing a row per crossing ([#1563](https://github.com/MatthewBonanni/mallard/issues/1563)).
