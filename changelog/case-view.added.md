- The case view draws the case: `mallard check --view DIR` also writes `DIR/case-light.svg` and `DIR/case-dark.svg`, annotated images drawn by Mallard itself (2D: the plane with the mesh; 3D: an isometric view over three orthographic ones) with every boundary zone colored by its condition and labeled with its name, condition and values in their units, every region, probe and periodic pair labeled by name, a legend, axes, a scale bar and the domain's extent in its units. The ParaView file adds the mesh's cells, the probes on points, lines and surfaces of their own, and each periodic pair with its faces' images; `mallard info MESH --view DIR` writes the view of a mesh alone (#546).
