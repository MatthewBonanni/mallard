- `[particles] type = "solid"`: inertial solid spheres of a given `diameter` and `density`, accelerated by drag (`drag = "stokes"` or `"schiller_naumann"`) toward the gas velocity of the reconstruction polynomial and by `gravity` with buoyancy; position and velocity are integrated by the flow's time integrator at its order, and particles whose relaxation time is below the step move at their equilibrium velocity, so tiny particles track the gas at any step without limiting it (#757).
- `[[particles.walls]]`: solid particles rebound off walls and symmetry planes with normal and tangential restitution coefficients per boundary zone, or stick to them and are counted as deposited (#757).
