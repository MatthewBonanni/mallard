- 2D TENO no longer settles into an odd-even wall-pressure mode on straight-sided curved walls with thin wall cells (NACA 0012 tutorial mesh, orders 3 and 4; orders 5 and 6 improve): mirror images are taken only across the boundary faces beneath their cells and on the target cell's side, and stencil candidates are ranked in the metric of the local mesh spacing as in 3D. 2D stencils on anisotropic or faceted-wall meshes change, and TENO cache files are recomputed (format version 11) (#382).
