- `viscous_gradients = "reconstruction"` costs less: TENO's smooth pass evaluates the gradients of its polynomials at the face points while it holds the coefficients, instead of a separate pass over the faces that re-read every coefficient. A step of the 3D Taylor-Green vortex with TENO5 on an A100: 240 to 184 ms on 96^3 hexahedra (least squares: 110 ms), 149 to 125 ms on 48^3 x 6 tetrahedra (93 ms); TENO6 408 to 301 and 222 to 174 ms. CPU results are the same bit for bit; on GPUs they differ by round-off, as the old pass's arithmetic was contracted. The gradients take 5.3 KB per cell for TENO5 on hexahedra, 2.7 KB on tetrahedra, against 1.4 KB for the coefficients (#1181).
