- The solvers are `mallard2d` and `mallard3d` (test suites `mallard-test2d`, `mallard-test3d`), installed side by side, and `mallard` runs the one a case needs: by the dimension of its mesh file (Gmsh, Fluent, CGNS, OpenFOAM, HDF5) or, for a generated mesh, 3D when `[mesh]` sets `Nz` or `Lz`; `mallard --dim 2|3` states it. `mallard -i`, `check`, `new <template>` and `mesh convert` pick the dimension; `ctl`, `reactor`, `upgrade-input` and `--version` run either solver; `mallard test` runs every installed test suite (`--dim` for one). A case whose solver is not installed is an error naming it and `-DMallard_DIM`. `Mallard_EXECUTABLE_SUFFIX` is removed (#483).
