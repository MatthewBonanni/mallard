- `[particles] type = "droplet"`: evaporating droplets of a one-component liquid (`[particles.liquid]`: vapor species of the mechanism, density, specific heat, latent heat, boiling and critical points, Clausius-Clapeyron or Antoine saturation pressure) in a viscous gas mixture, with the d^2-law or Abramzon-Sirignano (default) evaporation rates from the mechanism's film properties, boiling above the boiling temperature, and removal below `minimum_diameter`; with `coupling = "two-way"` the gas receives the vapor, its momentum and its energy, conserving mass, species, momentum and energy to round-off; restart files carry the droplets exactly (#760).
- Particle expressions read the particle diameter `D_P`, and droplet expressions the gas temperature `T` and droplet temperature `T_P` in a mixture; the output field `PARTICLE_SOURCE_RHOY_<species>` writes the droplets' vapor source (#760).
