- Flamelet output (#631): runs with `gas = "flamelet"` write the table's species (`Y_<species>`), its temperature (`T_TABLE`) and, when the table has one, its heat release rate (`HRR`) at each cell's `Z` and `C`. `MIXTURE_FRACTION` and `C_NORM` (the table's normalized progress variable, its axis `Lambda` as FPVgen names it) need no `[output.mixture]`. A run whose initial or boundary pressure differs from the table's by more than 10% warns `W-TABLE-PRESSURE`.
