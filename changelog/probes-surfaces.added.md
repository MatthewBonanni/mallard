- Surface probes: `shape = "cut_plane"` samples the exact intersection of a plane (a line in 2D) with the mesh, one sample per cut cell, with integrals exact for the reconstruction polynomials; in 3D, resampled `rectangle`, `disk`, `sphere`, `cylinder`, `cone` and parametric `surface` grids. Surfaces, cut planes and 2D curves have the flux variables of boundary probes along their normal, so `reductions = ["integral"]` gives mass, momentum and enthalpy flow through a section (#318, #395).
