- Steps decided on the device and enqueued ahead of the GPU (`[run] step_queue`, 3 steps by default), so CUDA graph runs launch the next step without waiting for the previous one. The device computes dt, t, the step's end, error-control retries, the NaN check, p_max, the conservation ledger and each progress row's values (with the run health and the range check of boundary values of `t`), the force and integral monitors (with the kinetic-energy budget), probes written by step or by time and the samples of time-averaged probes, the `[statistics]` means and batches, spectra, phase averages and conditional statistics (with the derived fields, `HRR` and `OMEGA_<species>` they read), and evaluates `[[triggers]]`, `wall` included; their reductions over the ranks use NCCL in NCCL builds. The device stops for the host only at a step it must act on: an output, a probe reading `[statistics]` outputs, a trigger that fires, a row of a `[statistics.convergence]` signal that a trigger reads, a checkpoint, a request (a signal, `mallard ctl`, a control file, the wall time), the end of the run or a divergence. Results and files are the same bit for bit at any depth. Steps ahead of the host need the conditional nodes of CUDA graphs (CUDA 12.4 or later); runs with triggers that read a probe's value, and the progress rows, monitors, means and probes of reacting runs with fused half steps, take one step at a time (#730, #906, #914).
