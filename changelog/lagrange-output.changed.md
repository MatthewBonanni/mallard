- **Probe values with `sampling = "point"` change in TENO troubled cells**: where they took linear least squares, they now come from the polynomial the scheme's stencil selection chooses for the whole cell (per variable, the central polynomial where it passes the flux's cutoff, else the mean of the sector polynomials that do, scaled as the flux's with `bound_preserving`), as the high-order view's nodes do; on coarse meshes of smooth fields, which flag many cells troubled, samples follow the field inside those cells instead of a straight line. `mallard post` keeps the sector stencils when it samples points. Step results are unchanged.
