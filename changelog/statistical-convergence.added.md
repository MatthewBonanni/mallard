- `[statistics.convergence]` follows monitored values (integrals, forces, probes) during the run: the end of the initial transient by MSER-5, confidence intervals on their means and variances by batch means (Student's t), integrated autocorrelation times and effective sample sizes, written to `convergence.csv` and the run record and carried by restart files. Triggers read them (`convergence_stationary`, `convergence_converged`, `convergence_<signal>_ci`, ...), so a run can start its statistics once the transient has ended and stop once the intervals reach a tolerance (#337).
