- `mallard diff A B` compares two snapshot, restart, probe or CSV files, of any format each: per field the values that differ bit for bit, the L1, L2 and Linf norms of B - A in the field's units and relative to A, and the cell (with its coordinates) and values of the largest difference; for restart files also the step, time, attributes, arrays and face records, bit for bit. `--tol` sets a relative tolerance per field and the exit status (0 within it, 1 beyond, 3 not comparable) makes it a regression check for scripts and CI; `--remap` compares files on different meshes through the conservative remap; `--format json` prints it for scripts.
