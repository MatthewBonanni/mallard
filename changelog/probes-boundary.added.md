- Boundary probes: `[[probes]]` with `boundary = "<zone>"` (or a list) or `boundary_type = "wall"` sample every face of the zones, as ordered polylines with arc length in 2D and polygons in 3D: the face states, convective fluxes (`MASS_FLUX`, `MOMENTUM_FLUX`, `ENTHALPY_FLUX`, `SPECIES_FLUX_*`), tractions, wall shear `TAU_W`, heat flux `Q_W`, `CF` and `CP` of a `reference` state, `U_TAU` and `Y_PLUS`, with the same output formats and reductions as other probes. Probe geometry also takes a `pivot` for `scale` and `rotate`, and in 3D `rotate = {axes = [...]}` (#318, #390).
