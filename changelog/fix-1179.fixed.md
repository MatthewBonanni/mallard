- `inlet_state` with `nscbc` no longer takes transverse terms in its incoming wave by default (`beta = 0` on every inlet, not only turbulent ones; outlets keep the local Mach number). The terms held the inlet's normal velocity off its target by `beta T- / (K rho c)`, which grows as the inverse of the cell size where the target steps along the inlet: two streams meeting at the inlet, as in the V11 mixing layer, drove spurious velocities at the step, up to three times the step on fine meshes. An explicit `beta` still applies. Runs with an `nscbc` inlet whose faces had transverse terms (2D and 3D inlets away from walls) change bitwise; one-cell-wide inlets, such as the `forced_inlet` and `flame_outflow` examples, are unchanged (#1179).
