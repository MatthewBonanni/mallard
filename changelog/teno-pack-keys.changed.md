- **Faster TENO setup packing with shared tables.** Packing only: each batch ranks, hashes and compares a stencil shape key's table once, at its first cell there, instead of at every cell of the key, on every backend. The packing of a 64^3 Taylor-Green vortex at TENO5 drops from 28-41 s to about 12 s on 2 threads, and on an A100 by 10-20%. Tables, cache files and results are bitwise unchanged (#1079).
