- **Faster keyed TENO setup on the host backends.** With shared tables in Serial, Threads and OpenMP builds, the cells that take a stencil shape key's tables no longer carry their own copy through the packing: each batch ranks, hashes and compares a key's table once, at its first cell, and the cells walk only the vertex-neighbor layers their stencils use to map the key's stencil. The packing of a 64^3 Taylor-Green vortex at TENO5 drops from 14-26 s to about 1.4 s on 2 threads. Tables, cache files and results are bitwise unchanged (#1079).
