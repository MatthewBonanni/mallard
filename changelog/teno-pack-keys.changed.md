- **Faster keyed TENO setup, now in every build.** With shared tables, the cells that take a stencil shape key's tables no longer carry their own copy through the packing: each batch ranks, hashes and compares a key's table once, at its first cell, and the cells walk only the vertex-neighbor layers their stencils use to map the key's stencil. GPU builds now key too instead of fitting every cell. The packing of a 64^3 Taylor-Green vortex at TENO5 drops from 45-48 s to 2.6-4.7 s on 2 threads; on one A100 the tables of 1M tetrahedra drop from 23.5 s to 0.2 s and setup from 34 s to 16-18 s. Tables, cache files and results are bitwise unchanged (#1079).
