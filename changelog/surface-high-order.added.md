- Region snapshots of TENO runs are drawn from the polynomials: boundary zones, cut planes, and surfaces and curves of `[regions]` (isosurfaces included) write each element as a VTK Lagrange cell of the reconstruction polynomials' degree (a triangle or quadrilateral in 3D, a curve in 2D; other polygons as fans of triangles), whose nodes hold the polynomial's values of the element's cell (the wall-side cell on a boundary), and `VORTICITY`, `Q_CRITERION`, `LAMBDA2`, `SCHLIEREN` and the other fields of the velocity gradient from its derivatives there. On by default; `high_order = false` writes one value per element. Statistics (`MEAN_*`) and other cell fields stay the value of the element's cell; the files are the same on any number of ranks ([#1509](https://github.com/MatthewBonanni/mallard/issues/1509)).
