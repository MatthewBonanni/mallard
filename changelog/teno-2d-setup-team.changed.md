- **2D TENO setup**: each thread sums the smoothness-indicator matrix one row at a time in registers instead of adding every term to its scratch in global memory, and on GPUs the fits' Householder sums and Lebesgue constants likewise; on NVIDIA GPUs the setup kernel runs four blocks of 128 threads per SM. The device tables take 2.8-3.3 times less time at order 6 on an A100 (4.8 s instead of 13.5 s on a 2.1M-triangle mesh) and 15-32% less at orders 3 and 4; the host backends' 2D tables take 2-43% less. The tables are bitwise the same (on CUDA in builds with `nvcc_wrapper` as the compiler; not yet with the host compiler, #1184).
