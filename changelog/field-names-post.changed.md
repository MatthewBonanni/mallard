- Post-processing outputs name their columns in uppercase, like fields: `mallard post average --region` writes `INTEGRAL_MEAN_<a>` and `MEAN_MEAN_<a>`, its profiles `N_CELLS`; `mallard post flame` writes `X_F`, `DELTA_T`, `DTDX_MAX`, `S_C`, `S_D`, `U_U`, `T_U`, `T_B`, `RHO_U`, `Y_<k>_U`, `Y_<k>_B` (also the keys of `--format json`); `mallard reactor` writes `P`, and `mallard input upgrade` notes it for a `[reactor]` input.
