- `mallard check` reports the resolution against the case's physical scales: the cell sizes; the Kolmogorov and Taylor lengths from the dissipation of the initial (or restart) field or of the `mass_flow` forcing; the friction velocity and the walls' first-cell y+, x+ and z+ from a restart's wall shear, Dean's channel correlation (`mass_flow`) or a flat plate's skin friction; the wall model's exchange points in wall units; cells across TFLES's `delta_L`. Each states its assumption, or why it is not estimated. Warnings, with the spacing to aim for, when DNS cells exceed 2 Kolmogorov lengths, first cells y+ 1, wall cells x+ 15 / z+ 8 (DNS) or 150 / 40 (wall-resolved LES), or a wall model's exchange point sits below y+ 30; `--quality` also writes their fields `Y_PLUS`, `DELTA_OVER_ETA` and `CELLS_PER_DELTA_L` (#548).
