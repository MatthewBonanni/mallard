- `mallard post flame`: the position, thermal thickness (and the largest temperature gradient), consumption speed and displacement speed of a premixed flame, planar on average, in a run's snapshots or restart files, per snapshot and over the end of the series, with the run's own production rates (the snapshots need no `OMEGA_*` fields) and the errors against a reference flame speed. The consumed species follows from the fresh gas's equivalence ratio (the fuel up to stoichiometric, else the oxidizer), and `--burnt-temperature` gives the thickness against an equilibrium burnt temperature (#942).
