- `mallard post signal FILE --column NAME`: the spectrum (Welch, with `--segment`, `--overlap`, `--window`, `--detrend`, the refined peak, its amplitude and `--strouhal`), the autocorrelation with Sokal's integrated time, and MSER-5's end of the transient with the mean, RMS and variance and their batch-means intervals and `--tolerance` verdict of any column of a probe, force or integral CSV file, by the code of the probes' time spectra and `[statistics.convergence]`; `--reference` adds a `[[forces]]` file's `CD` and `CL`. Units follow the file's units line; `-o DIR` writes CSV tables with units lines, `--format json` everything (schema `mallard-signal`), and `mallard.post.signal` runs it from Python ([#1362](https://github.com/MatthewBonanni/mallard/issues/1362)).
