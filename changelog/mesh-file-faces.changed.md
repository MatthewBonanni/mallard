- CGNS, Fluent and OpenFOAM meshes are read by every rank of a distributed run together, as Gmsh files are: each rank reads a near-equal share of the nodes and of the elements (CGNS, by partial reads) or faces (Fluent, OpenFOAM, whose faces go to the ranks of their cells to rebuild them), so no rank holds the whole mesh. TIMING. Meshes are bitwise unchanged and do not depend on the number of ranks. ASCII Fluent node and face sections and ASCII OpenFOAM lists must hold one item per line (short OpenFOAM lists may be on one line), as Fluent and OpenFOAM write them (#1124, #1000).
