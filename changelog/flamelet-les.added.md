- LES of flamelets (#287): `gas = "flamelet"` now runs with `[les]`. Every table lookup is at the cell's subgrid variance of `Z` from the algebraic closure `C_Z Delta^2 |grad Z|^2` (`physics.variance = "algebraic"`, `C_Z`, default 0.1), written as `MIXTURE_FRACTION_VARIANCE`. The eddy viscosity adds `mu_t / Sc_t` to the diffusion of `Z` and `C` and `mu_t / Pr_t` to the conduction of enthalpy.
