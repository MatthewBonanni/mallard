- Thickened flames with tabulated chemistry: `[les.combustion]` `model = "tfles"` combines with `gas = "flamelet"`, thickening the diffusion of `Z`, `C` and enthalpy by `E F` and the source of `C` (and `HRR`) by `E / F`, with the flame sensor and the dynamic efficiency on the table's normalized progress variable (`sensor = "progress"`, the default with the flamelet gas). The table is then read at zero subgrid variance of `Z`: `[physics]` `variance = "none"`, the default with `[les.combustion]` and also available in LES without it, looks every lookup up on the table's `Sz = 0` slice ([guide](https://github.com/MatthewBonanni/mallard/blob/main/docs/guide/flamelet.md#thickened-flames)).
