- One rank reading a CGNS or OpenFOAM mesh file needs less memory than before the files were read by every rank together: on 8M hexahedra, 1.7 GB instead of 2.2 GB for CGNS and 2.8 GB instead of 3.4-3.5 GB for OpenFOAM (2.8 and 4.5 GB in the development version). The readers free each face and cell list once it is sent, send the faces as 32-bit words when the mesh's node, face and cell counts fit, and give the cells their compact node numbers with one lookup per node instead of up to eight, so one rank also reads a CGNS file faster (3.2 s instead of 3.5 s; 5.7 s in the development version). On 3 ranks, the largest rank needs 0.7 GB instead of 1.0 GB for CGNS and 1.1 GB instead of 2.4 GB for OpenFOAM. Meshes are bitwise unchanged (#1158).
