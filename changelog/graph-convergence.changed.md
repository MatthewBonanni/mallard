- Triggers that read `[statistics.convergence]` values no longer stop the steps enqueued ahead of the GPU (`[run] step_queue`) at the rows of the convergence signals: the step graph updates the convergence estimate itself, from copies of the histories in device memory (32 bytes per sample, at least 4096 samples per signal), and reduces the probes the signals read. Results are the same bit for bit with any queue depth. The convergence estimate is now computed without contracted multiply-adds, so its values from compilers that contracted them (e.g. Clang on arm64) change at round-off (#1062).
