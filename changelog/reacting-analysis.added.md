- `[[statistics.conditional]]`: PDFs and joint PDFs of one or two variables (the mixture fraction, the progress variable, any `[statistics]` field) and conditional means and variances of fields on them, by volume or mass (Favre), optionally in a region, on the device; per-sample sums exact in fixed point, reduced over the ranks with NCCL or MPI, so the results are the same bit for bit on any number of ranks and across restarts. `mallard post average --conditional` computes them from saved snapshots, bit for bit the run's from restart files of its sampling steps (#513).
- `[statistics]` samples the heat release rate `HRR` and the production rates `OMEGA_<species>` of reacting runs, in means, covariances and conditional statistics (#513).
