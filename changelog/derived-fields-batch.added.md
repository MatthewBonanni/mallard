- Derived fields `P0`, `T0`, `H0` (stagnation pressure, temperature and enthalpy), `SOUND_SPEED`, `ENTROPY`, `DILATATION`, `HELICITY` (3D), `ENSTROPHY`, `STRAIN_RATE` and `CP` (with the new `[output] reference`), and for mixtures `MIXTURE_FRACTION` (Bilger), `PROGRESS_VARIABLE`, `FLAME_INDEX` (Takeno) and `SCALAR_DISSIPATION`, relative to the new `[output.mixture]` streams, species and progress definition; all from one catalog that `[[write_data]]`, `mallard post derive` and `mallard list fields` share (#511).
- `mallard post derive FILE... --fields LIST`: derived fields of restart files and snapshots after the run, from the run's own reconstruction and gradients, bit for bit what `[[write_data]]` writes at those steps from restart files; `--reference` and `--mixture` give the input a run lacked (#511).
- `[output] reference` is also the default `reference` of the wall probes' `CF` and `CP`; a probe's own replaces it.
