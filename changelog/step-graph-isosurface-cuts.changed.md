- Probes over isosurfaces that read normals (`MASS_FLUX` and the other fluxes) or `sampling = "point"` no longer stop the step queue at their writes when the surface is cut from one condition alone, unclipped, on a mesh without curved cells: the device cuts each crossed cell's level into the host's quadrature points bit for bit and samples them there, and the host cuts the staged levels again for the geometry. Polygons cut from 3D cells are ordered by an exact angular comparison instead of `atan2`, on the host as on the device.
- On GPUs, the device computes the levels, fractions and motions of regions (`region_motion.cpp`) without contracted multiply-adds, as the host does, so probes over `where` and moving regions can differ from earlier GPU runs in the last bits.
