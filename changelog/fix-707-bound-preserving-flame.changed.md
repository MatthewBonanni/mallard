- Bound-preserving TENO (`bound_preserving = true`) no longer makes premixed flames 4-6% too fast: it clipped troubled cells at smooth extrema of the nearly uniform pressure, whose overshoot is far below the pressure but not below its range among the neighbors, and fell to first order there. The neighbors' range of density and pressure is now widened by 0.1% of the cell's value; the stoichiometric H2/air flame at 700 K runs within 1% of Cantera with TENO3 and TENO5 at 3 and 6 cells per thermal thickness, as without it (#707).
