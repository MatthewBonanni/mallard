- Probes over moving regions no longer stop the steps enqueued ahead of the GPU (`[run] step_queue`) at their writes or averaging samples: the step graph updates the region's fractions at the device's `t`, selects the cells it covers and evaluates them into the probe's staged values, and the host orders and reduces them as it takes the record. Files are the same bit for bit. Each probe's pinned staging holds twice the cells the region covers when the graph is built; a write that covers more stops the queue once, the host writes it, and the graph is rebuilt with larger staging. Probes over regions that follow the solution still stop the queue (#1095).
