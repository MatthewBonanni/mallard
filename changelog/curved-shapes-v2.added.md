- `[[mesh.curved]]` projects a boundary zone onto the surface of any region: ellipses and ellipsoids, tori, cones, transformed primitives, booleans and implicit functions, by closest-point iterations on the region's function; circles, spheres and infinite cylinders without transforms keep their closed form, bit for bit. `examples/ellipse` (inviscid flow past an elliptic cylinder) and `make_cylinder_mesh.py --aspect` for elliptic O-grids (#407).
