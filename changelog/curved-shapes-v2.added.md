- `[[mesh.curved]]` projects a boundary zone onto a surface region (2D: a curve), the boundary of any filled region (`filled = false`) or a 2D `circle` or `ellipse`: ellipses and ellipsoids, tori, cones, transformed primitives, booleans and implicit functions, by closest-point iterations on the region's function; circles, spheres and infinite cylinders without transforms keep their closed form, bit for bit. (#407).
