- A species absent from a cell next to round-off in a neighbor (e.g. 1e-34 against 0) no longer sets the cell's shared limiter (MUSCL) or bound-preserving factor (TENO) of the mixture scalars to zero, which put every mass fraction, gamma and e0 of the cell at first order: such a species is reconstructed constant instead. On `flame_3d` this was a quarter of the cells, and results jumped by about 1 K with any change of the chemistry tolerances or `explicit_step` (#1169).
