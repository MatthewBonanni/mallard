- `mallard check` breaks the memory down by category (solution and stages, faces and gradients, reconstruction tables, mesh, boundaries and models, chemistry, diagnostics, output staging, halo buffers, work memory, setup) at the setup's peak and in the steady state, per cell and in total, with the device memory outside Kokkos (CUDA's context and kernels' local memory) on GPUs (#550).
- `mallard check --memory 40GB` or `--memory a100-80` (also `a100-40`, `h100-80`, `h200`, `mi250x`, `mi300x`; by default the GPU's own memory in GPU builds) sizes the run: the most cells per GPU or rank, the fewest GPUs the mesh fits on with the largest part and its peak (the larger of the setup's peak and the steady state), and up to how many the halo stays below 10% of the owned cells. On an A100, from a check of 4 to 16 times fewer cells, the predicted peak is within -1.0% to +3.3% of the measured one (#550).
