- 3D TENO runs whose troubled cells took more than one round (`troubled_capacity` below 1, the 3D default 0.5) could differ in the last bits from run to run, or between rank counts. Which cells the first round took followed the order of the queue's atomic increments, and those cells kept the smooth pass's central coefficients while the rest recomputed them in another kernel, whose FMAs could round differently (seen on GPUs with rotational periodicity). Every round now recomputes them.
