- The TENO smooth pass finds the central stencil entries of the cells of structured blocks by index arithmetic: a cell whose stencil holds no mirror image reads the cell plus one list of offsets shared by its block shape, instead of its own list of entries. Results are bitwise those of the unstructured path. The Numerics section of the log reports the share of the cells on this block path and the number of block shapes, e.g. `88.0% of the cells on the block path, 125 block shapes` (#440).
