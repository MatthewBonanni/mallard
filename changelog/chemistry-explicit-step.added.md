- `[chemistry] explicit_step = true` first advances each cell whose last RODAS sub-step covered the whole chemistry call by one explicit Bogacki–Shampine 3(2) step, with no Jacobian and no factorization, kept only if it passes RODAS's error test, stays admissible and is not stiff; other cells take RODAS as before. Results change within the chemistry's tolerances and stay bitwise independent of ranks, partition and step graphs; off by default (#298).
