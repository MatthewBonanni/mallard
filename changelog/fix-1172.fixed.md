- Single-precision builds fit one cell per TENO stencil shape again: the shape keys' grid of 2^-30 of a cell was finer than the round-off of float coordinates, so cells of one shape got keys of their own and nearly every cell was fitted (#1172). Single-precision keys are on a grid of a 16th of a cell, and each cell takes its shape's tables only if its geometry agrees with the fitted cell's to within 16 round-offs of the largest coordinate. Single-precision caches of shared tables from earlier versions are set up again; double-precision results and caches are unchanged.
