- **Usage errors exit with status 2** and print one line pointing at the command's `--help`: an unknown option (previously ignored by `mallard -i`, `check` and `reactor`), an unknown command, a missing argument. `mallard check`, `reactor` and `mesh convert` exited with 1 on these. Options take their value as `--opt VALUE` or `--opt=VALUE`; `--version` is accepted only as the first argument (#503).
