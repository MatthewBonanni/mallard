- `viscous_gradients = "reconstruction"` now serves gas mixtures: the temperature and mole fraction gradients of the heat, species diffusion, Soret and enthalpy fluxes come from TENO's polynomials of `W` and the mass fractions at each face point, as the velocity gradients. The species diffusion residual of a manufactured binary mixture converges at third order on triangles with TENO5 (least squares: first). Walls of mixtures keep least-squares gradients; flamelet gases are rejected (#1145).
