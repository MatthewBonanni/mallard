- Open STL and OBJ surfaces are surface regions (2D: curves, their section by z = 0) with `filled = false`, e.g. `{type = "stl", file = "baffle.stl", units = "mm", filled = false}` for probes, fluxes and snapshots on a sheet: their own triangles, cut into patches about half a cell wide, each in the cell holding its center, so the area is exact; each connected piece faces as its first triangle in the file by the right-hand rule (`flip = true` reverses it). An open surface without `filled = false`, or where a filled region is needed, is an error that says it encloses nothing; its rim, clipping by regions of the solution and curved zones on it are errors that say they are not supported yet ([#1302](https://github.com/MatthewBonanni/mallard/issues/1302)).
