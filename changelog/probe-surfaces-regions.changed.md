- Cut-plane probes come from the shared region code (`region_mesh`): the plane's exact cut of each cell's simplices, clipped to `bounds` by six half spaces, with the same degree-5 quadrature; results equal to round-off. The performance set gains `probes_3d`, the `teno5_3d` case with probes every step, measured over time stepping and output (#499).
- Probe surfaces are regions: `[[probes]]` `region = {type = "sphere", ..., filled = false}` (or any surface of one primitive, or a flat surface) with `n_points = [n_u, n_v]` is resampled on a grid along the region's parameters (listed per type in the geometry guide; cylinders and cones with their ends), replacing the probe types `sphere`, `cylinder`, `cone`, `disk` and `rectangle` of #572 (unreleased), which are now errors naming the region; the parametric `type = "surface"` stays. 3D `disk` regions take `inner_radius` (#499).
- Region probes over surfaces and curves sample at their elements' quadrature points: `sampling = "point"` and fluxes (`MASS_FLUX`, ...) through surfaces (2D: curves) (#499).
