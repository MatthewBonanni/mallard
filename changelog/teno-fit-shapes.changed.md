- **TENO setup fits one cell per stencil shape.** With shared tables (the default), cells are grouped by a key of their stencil's geometry (the cells, nodes and boundary faces their search reads, relative to the cell and in its units) before any table is fitted; the cell with the lowest global id of each key is fitted and the others take its stencil and tables. Lattice regions set up in a fraction of the time (#986). Results change once at round-off where two cells of one shape used to fall on either side of the table comparison's grid, and stay bitwise independent of the rank count, partition and thread count. TENO cache files are recomputed once (format 16).
