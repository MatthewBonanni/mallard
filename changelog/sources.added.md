- `[[sources]]`: any number of mass, momentum, energy and species sources, each a number, quantity or expression of position, time, the local solution (`rho`, `u_x`, `p`, `T`, `Y_<species>`, ...) and domain averages (`mean_ke`, ...), restricted to a [region](docs/input.md#regions) and optionally given as an exact integrated total (`total = true`); evaluated on the device every stage without leaving CUDA graphs. `[[write_data]]` writes them as `SOURCE_*` (#317, #384).
- Examples `source_regions` and `forced_isotropic_turbulence` (linear forcing at a target kinetic energy) (#384).
