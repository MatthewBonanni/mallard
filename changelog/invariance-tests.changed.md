- **Analytical initial states on quadrilaterals change by their quadrature error (up to 1e-8 relative for states that vary strongly across coarse cells):** cells are averaged over the fan of triangles from their vertex average rather than from their first node, so that they do not depend on the node order of the mesh file (#436).
- **TENO results change in troubled cells:** the smoothness indicators weight each derivative by its multinomial coefficient, and in 3D the two shear waves of a face select their stencils together, so that stencil selection depends neither on the orientation of the axes nor, in 3D, on the cell numbering. TENO cache files of earlier versions are recomputed. There is no switch back (#436).
