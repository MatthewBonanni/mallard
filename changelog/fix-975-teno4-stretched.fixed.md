- TENO-E orders 4 and 6 no longer amplify acoustic waves at order-one rates on graded 2D meshes at the default `stencil_factor`, such as the coarse cylinder O-grid at order 4 (largest Mach number 1.02 in a Mach 0.2 flow, now 0.34 as at order 5): 2D odd-degree stencils rank their candidates in the mesh-spacing metric on every cell, not only where the spacing ratio exceeds 2.5. This is a mitigation: slow modes (up to 1e-3 sound speeds per box length) remain, and other stencil sizes can be worse than before (order 4 at `stencil_factor` 1.6 or less, order 6 at 2.2 and 2.6). Errors on `cartesian_tri` grow 1.4 times at order 4 and 4 times at order 6. TENO cache files are rewritten (format 20) (#975).
