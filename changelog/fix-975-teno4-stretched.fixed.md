- TENO-E orders 4 to 6 no longer amplify acoustic waves at order-one rates on graded 2D meshes at the default `stencil_factor`, such as the coarse cylinder O-grid at order 4 (largest Mach number 1.02 in a Mach 0.2 flow, now 0.34 as at order 5) and a planar cylinder O-grid at order 5 (wall entropy error 6e-2, now 7e-4): odd-degree stencils (orders 4 and 6) on every 2D cell, and order-5 stencils on 2D quads, rank their candidates in the mesh-spacing metric, not only where the spacing ratio exceeds 2.5. This is a mitigation: slow modes (up to 1e-2 sound speeds per length) remain, and other stencil sizes can be worse than before or still grow (1.6 or less at orders 4 and 6, 1.5 at order 5, 2.2 and 2.6 at order 6). Errors on `cartesian_tri` grow 1.4 times at order 4 and 4 times at order 6 (order 5 on triangles is unchanged), and up to 3.2 times at curved walls. TENO cache files are rewritten (format 20) (#975).
