- Snapshots of regions: `[[write_data]]` `region = ...` (or `regions = [...]`, a series per region) writes a volume as the subset of cells it covers, with only their nodes and `REGION_FRACTION`, a boundary zone's faces, and a cut plane, resampled surface, curve or points as the point values a `sampling = "point"` probe of it writes (cell fields from each sample's cell), in VTKHDF or VTK files, the same on any number of ranks; every region is written again at the end of the run. `[regions]` and the `region` keys of `[[probes]]` and `[[write_data]]` also take regions of lower dimension (`type = "cut_plane"`, `"line"`, `"points"`, ... with the probe shapes' keys), boundary zones by name and `"domain"` (#542).
