- Run health events in the run log, with stable codes (#1344): `W-HEALTH-CFL-LIMIT` and `W-HEALTH-CFL-SPIKE` (a step's CFL number above the integrator's limit, or twice its recent median), `W-HEALTH-DT-COLLAPSE` (the stable time step below a tenth of its recent median), `W-HEALTH-REJECTED-STEPS` (over a quarter of the steps rejected by the error controller), `W-HEALTH-JOB-TIME` (the Slurm job, `SLURM_JOB_END_TIME`, ends before the run), `W-HEALTH-NONFINITE` (a monitored probe, force or integral value that is NaN or infinite), and a `wall_time` event at 90% of `t_wall_stop`. Each warning record has the `step`, `t` and the `values` it judged. They are judged on the host from each step's time step and the progress rows: nothing is added to the step.
- `heartbeat` records in the run log every `[output] heartbeat_interval` of wall time (default 60 s), so that a hung run can be told from a slow one.
- `health.residuals` in the progress records: the change of the state over the step, relative to the state (`rho`, `momentum`, `energy`). `[output] steady_residual` adds a `converged` event once every residual is below it, and `W-HEALTH-STALL` when the largest has not halved in `[output] stall_steps` steps.
- The conservation ledger's warning has its own code, `W-HEALTH-LEDGER` (it was `W-HEALTH-MARGIN`); `W-HEALTH-MARGIN` and `W-HEALTH-LEDGER` records have the `step` and `t`.
