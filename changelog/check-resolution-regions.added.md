- `mallard check`'s resolution report gives its values per region too: cell sizes, first-cell y+, the region's own dissipation, Kolmogorov length and cells per Kolmogorov length, for each region of the new `[check] regions` (by default every cell zone of the mesh), with a `W-DNS-ETA` warning of its own for a region short of the DNS target (#771).
- `[check] statistics` takes the dissipation from an earlier run's `[statistics]` averages (`STRAIN_RATE` with its square, or `ENSTROPHY`) in a restart file or a snapshot of the whole mesh, instead of the initial state's or the forcing's, and gives `DELTA_OVER_ETA` each cell's own Kolmogorov length (#771).
