- `mallard post extract --probe NAME` without `-i` reads boundary probes from a run's recorded input: the resolved input gave boundary probes, and `[[write_data]]` snapshots of a boundary zone or of a region's elements, a `type = ""` that the probe reader then rejected ("boundary probes take no type"). With probe `type` an optional key with no default, the recorded input leaves it out of them and reads back as the input it resolves (#1360).
