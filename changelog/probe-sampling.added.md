- Probe files record where each sample is read: a probe sampling block (`mallard-probe-sampling`, a `# mallard-probe-sampling` line of CSV files, the root attribute `mallard_sampling` of HDF5 and VTKHDF files, a comment of VTP files) holds the sampling mode, the reconstruction (method, order, fallbacks), each column's value kind and average, per point the requested location, owning cell, its centroid and the distance to where the value is read, and for clipped probes the samples dropped outside the mesh. `mallard info` prints it and `tools/mallard/vtu.py` has `probe_sampling()` (#673).
