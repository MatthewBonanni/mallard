- `[les] gradients = "reconstruction"` (TENO only, off by default): the subgrid models take the velocity gradient of TENO's central polynomial in its smooth cells instead of the second-order least-squares gradient, which halves the eddy viscosity on coarse meshes. Decaying isotropic turbulence with TENO5 and HLLC: spectral error 0.112 / 0.113 -> 0.091 / 0.094 at 32^3; the reconstruction computes the gradients in its own pass (about 7% more time per step) (#660).
