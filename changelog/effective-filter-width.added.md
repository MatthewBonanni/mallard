- `[les] effective_width = true` (experimental, off by default; TENO only): the widths of the subgrid models (eddy viscosity, PaSR mixing time, thickened flame `F_max` and efficiency) are multiplied in each cell where TENO uses its central polynomial by the ratio of that stencil's effective resolution to MUSCL's, measured from the modified wavenumbers of the real reconstructions (`tools/docs/effective_resolution.py`; 0.74 in 2D and 0.56 in 3D at order 5), and by 1 in troubled cells, relaxed over about 10 steps; output variable `LES_WIDTH_RATIO`, carried by restart files (#660).
