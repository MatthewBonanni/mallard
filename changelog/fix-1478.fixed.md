- Particle deposition with a `deposition_width` also deposits each particle's second moments, so the spread kernel's covariance is `deposition_width^2` in every direction wherever the particle sits in its cell, instead of falling short by `d d^T` for an offset `d` from the cell center (#1478). The deposit's error no longer depends on the in-cell position (8 to 37 h^2 before, about 8 h^2 now at `deposition_width = 0.08` on Cartesian meshes), so convergence studies at a fixed particle position converge evenly. Deposited sources and dispersed-phase fields of runs with a width change bitwise from earlier versions.
