- `[chemistry] integrator = "bdf"`: a variable-order BDF chemistry integrator (orders 1-5, Jacobian and factorization kept over many sub-steps), on the device like RODAS, with the dense or sparse LU. On n-dodecane and n-hexane its throughput is 1.0-1.7 times RODAS's on an A100 and on CPUs, most at `dt` = 1e-8 s; at the default `atol` its ignition delays are less accurate (H2: lower `atol` to 1e-14). RODAS stays the default and is unchanged (#143).
