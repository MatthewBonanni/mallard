- Exact global sums (the dynamic LES constant and TFLES efficiency, source-term integrals and means, probes reduced on the device, particle statistics) are now the exact sum rounded once to the nearest double, accumulated in 32-bit integer limbs, instead of each term rounded to a fixed point set by the largest term. Across ranks they take one all-reduce instead of two, which saves a network round trip per sum on several nodes. Results are still bitwise independent of the rank count, partition and backend, but their last bits differ from earlier versions: each sum is now at least as accurate as before, and usually more (#1097).
