- `mallard post` commands (`extract`, `derive`, `average`) set TENO up with its central stencils alone, which gradients and point samples read: no sector stencils, smoothness matrices, moments or troubled-cell scratch, and no species reconstruction of mixtures, unless they write `STENCIL_*` fields, which keep the run's full setup; that setup on CPUs works in batches of 2,048 cells instead of 8,192. `post derive` of a 64³ TENO5 Taylor-Green vortex peaks at 1.1 GB instead of 2.2 GB and takes about a quarter less CPU time; derived and stencil fields are bitwise unchanged. `mallard post` no longer rewrites the run's `[output] write_stencils` file (#815).
