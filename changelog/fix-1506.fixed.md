- Exact surfaces (analytic primitives and STL/OBJ surfaces) keep their whole area on hexahedral, prism and pyramid meshes with warped faces: point location tested such cells against the planes of their faces, which leave gaps along the cells' edges, so surface samples there belonged to no cell and were dropped (up to 0.1% of a surface's area on a jittered hexahedral mesh). Cells with warped faces are now the tetrahedra that define their volumes, which neighboring cells share; probes and particles placed in those gaps are now located in their cell instead of the nearest centroid (#1506).
