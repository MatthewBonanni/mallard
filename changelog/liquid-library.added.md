- Named liquids for droplets: `[particles.liquid] fluid = "water"` (or ammonia, ethanol, methane, methanol, n-decane, n-dodecane, n-heptane, n-hexane, n-octane, n-pentane, nitrogen, oxygen, propane, toluene) gives the droplets the density, specific heat, latent heat, saturation pressure, viscosity, conductivity and surface tension of the saturated liquid as functions of temperature, tabulated from CoolProp at setup to `tolerance` (default 1e-6) and read on the device; any `[particles.liquid]` key replaces its table with a constant. Needs the new build option `Mallard_ENABLE_COOLPROP` (off by default), which builds CoolProp with the host compiler.
- Particle expressions read the particle's density `RHO_P` and a droplet liquid's viscosity `MU_P`, conductivity `K_P` and surface tension `SIGMA_P`; `[particles.liquid]` takes constant `viscosity`, `conductivity` and `surface_tension`.
