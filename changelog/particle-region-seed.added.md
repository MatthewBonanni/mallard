- Particle injectors with `count = N` (or a total `mass` of solid particles) release N parcels at once, at random in a region, on boundary zones or at a point, at the run's start (with the seeds, before the first step) or at a later `start`; the same particles on any number of ranks, and a restarted run does not release them again. Injected solid particles given no `velocity`/`speed` start at the gas velocity where they enter, and without a `temperature` (here or in `[particles]`) at the gas temperature, as seeds do. The particles guide's new Seeding overview lists every way particles enter.
