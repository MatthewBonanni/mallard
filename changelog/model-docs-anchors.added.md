- The key catalog (`mallard list keys`, `mallard check --catalog`, `docs/input-keys/`) gives, for each value that names a model from the literature, the theory section with its formulation and its source (`models`, e.g. `particles.drag`: `singh` at `docs/theory/particles.md#singh-drag`, Singh et al. 2022): drag laws and particle forces, SGS models, filter width, wall models, Riemann solvers, convective fluxes, reconstructions, limiters, time integrators, viscosity laws, transport models, the chemistry integrator and coupling, radiation, thickened-flame models and efficiencies, PaSR and synthetic-turbulence spectra. The values of `les.filter_width`, `les.combustion.model`, `efficiency` and `subgrid_velocity`, `physics.transport`, `chemistry.integrator` and `coupling`, and `radiation.model` are now listed. The theory manual gains anchors for each model, a subsection on the further particle forces and one on the spectra of synthetic initial turbulence.
