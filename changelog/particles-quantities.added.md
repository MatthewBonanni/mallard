- `[[particles.quantities]]`: values each particle follows along its path, the time integral, maximum, minimum or first time of an expression of the gas at the particle, its position, time, velocity, earlier quantities and `in(region)`, and its turns about an axis (`winding`), optionally gated by `while`; integrals and windings are integrated by the flow's time integrator at its order, and quantities are written to `[particles] file`, continue across restarts and are bitwise the same on any rank count (#756).
- `[[particles.probes]]`: the count, mean, standard deviation, minimum and maximum of particle expressions over the particles in a region, every `interval` steps, in a CSV file, bitwise the same on any rank count (#756).
