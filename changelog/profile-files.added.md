- Profile files describe themselves: a comment line `# mallard-profile {...}` before a CSV file's header gives the defaults of its data entry's `coordinates`, `at`, `units` and `reference`, and records its `source` and `transforms`, so that `[[boundaries.data]]` and `[[initialize.data]]` entries need only a name and a file ([#1107](https://github.com/MatthewBonanni/mallard/issues/1107)).
- `scale` rescales a data entry to new `length`, `velocity`, `density` and `temperature` scales from its `reference` ones, each column by its dimension (Reynolds stresses as the velocity squared, pressures as `rho U^2`), so one boundary-layer profile serves inlets of any thickness and free stream; `columns` picks the value columns to read, and `outside = "error"` makes faces beyond a boundary table's range an error instead of holding its end values ([#1107](https://github.com/MatthewBonanni/mallard/issues/1107)).
- `turbulence.profile` reads its precursor statistics as a data entry, in any boundary coordinate: it names a `[[boundaries.data]]` entry with the columns `MEAN_U_*` and `COV_U_*_U_*` (read at the wall distance `n_m` of a turned inlet, in other units, rescaled), or a file, read as such an entry whose first column may now be a local coordinate as well as `x`, `y`, `z` ([#1107](https://github.com/MatthewBonanni/mallard/issues/1107)).
