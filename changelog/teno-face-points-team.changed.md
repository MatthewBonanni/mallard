- 3D TENO no longer stores the quadrature points of planar faces: the kernels (and the viscous flux of `viscous_gradients = "reconstruction"`) place them from the faces' nodes with the setup's roundings. On meshes without curved faces TENO5 needs 600 B less device memory per hexahedron and 310 B per tetrahedron (10% and 5% more cells on an A100-80GB), results are bitwise unchanged, and the time per step falls by about 1% (21% in a blast, whose troubled cells read the points most) (#978).
