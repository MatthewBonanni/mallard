- 3D TENO no longer stores the quadrature points of planar faces: the kernels place them from the faces' nodes with the setup's roundings. On meshes without curved faces TENO5 needs 580 B less device memory per hexahedron and 330 B per tetrahedron (8.5% and 4.3% more cells on an A100-80GB), results are bitwise unchanged, and the time per step falls by 1-3% (17% in a blast, whose troubled cells read the points most) (#978).
