- **Boundary options and values** (#564): every boundary type takes its options as keys — `nscbc = { L, sigma, beta, sigma_T, sigma_t }` (non-reflecting), `average = true`, `turbulence`, `wall_model` — and rejects keys and options it does not take with an error naming the type that takes them (``q` doesn't apply to `wall_isothermal`; use `wall_heat_flux``) and, for combinations not supported yet, the plan (#565). The wall temperature of `wall_isothermal`, the heat flux of `wall_heat_flux`, the pressure of `outlet_pressure` (of `t` only with `average`) and the free stream of `farfield` may be expressions of x, y, z, t. Vectors (`u` of inlets, walls and `farfield`, `mass_flux`) are given as components or as a magnitude along `direction` (default: each face's inward normal); a `mass_flux` vector sets the direction and each face's flux. The guide has a section per type and option, and a support matrix that a test compares with the parser.
- **Examples** `heated_wall` (a heat-flux wall whose box gains exactly `q A t`) and `average_outlet` (an area-averaged outlet with subsonic and supersonic faces) (#564).
