- Cell fractions of regions with flat faces (rectangles, boxes, half-spaces, polygons and booleans of them, without a smooth `edge`) are exact to rounding: cells are cut along the faces instead of sampled. Where faces smaller than a cell need more cuts than a cell allows, that part of the cell is sampled as before. `REGION_FRACTION`, and the weights of sources, sponges and statistics over such regions, change in the cells the faces cross, by up to about 5e-3 (for faces aligned with Cartesian cells) toward the exact value; regions with curved surfaces are unchanged. Setting them up is much faster: 64 box writers on a 48³ mesh take 0.4 s instead of 25 s (#1173).
