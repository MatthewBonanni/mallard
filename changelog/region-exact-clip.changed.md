- Cell fractions of regions with flat faces (rectangles, boxes, half-spaces, polygons and booleans of them, without a smooth `edge`) are exact: cells are cut along the faces instead of sampled. `REGION_FRACTION`, and the weights of sources, sponges and statistics over such regions, change in the cells the faces cross, by up to about 2e-4; regions with curved surfaces are unchanged. Setting them up is much faster: 64 box writers on a 48³ mesh take 1.6 s instead of 96 s (#1173).
