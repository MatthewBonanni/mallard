- MUSCL reads the gradient weights and face-centroid offsets of every cell of its face-neighbor fit (not tetrahedra, no face on a characteristic boundary) without a rotational periodic face from a table row, instead of solving its least-squares system every step: 4-5% more cells/s on lattices on an A100. Cells whose rows are equal to about 1e-9 of their magnitude (4e-6 in single precision) share the row of the lowest global id, agreed over the ranks; when fewer than 8 cells share each row (no lattice), MUSCL keeps no rows and fits every step as before. On lattices MUSCL results change once in the last bits; on meshes whose cells differ by less than the sharing grid (e.g. coordinate jitter below about 1e-9 relative), each cell uses its row's owner's weights, a change of that relative size. Results stay bitwise independent of the partition, and the run summary reports MUSCL's rows (#1035). MUSCL no longer contracts multiplies and adds into FMAs, so its fused kernel and its separate gradient, limiter and face kernels give the same face states bit for bit on GPUs; MUSCL results on GPUs change once in the last bits.
