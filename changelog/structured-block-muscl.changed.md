- MUSCL reads the gradient weights and face-centroid offsets of every cell whose faces all have a neighbor (no boundary face, no rotational periodic face; not tetrahedra) from a table row shared by the cells with equal rows and agreed over the ranks, instead of solving its least-squares system every step. The cells of structured blocks take their neighbors by offsets. MUSCL results change once in the last bits and stay bitwise independent of the partition; the block path gives the unstructured path's results bitwise, and the Numerics section of the log reports it for MUSCL too (#1035).
