- MUSCL reads the gradient weights and face-centroid offsets of every cell of its face-neighbor fit (not tetrahedra, no face on a characteristic boundary) without a rotational periodic face from a table row, instead of solving its least-squares system every step. Cells whose rows are equal to about 1e-9 of their magnitude share the row of the lowest global id, agreed over the ranks; when fewer than 8 cells share each row (no lattice), MUSCL keeps no rows and fits every step as before. The cells of structured blocks take their neighbors by offsets. On lattices MUSCL results change once in the last bits; on meshes whose cells differ by less than the sharing grid (e.g. coordinate jitter below about 1e-9 relative), each cell uses its row's owner's weights, a change of that relative size. Results stay bitwise independent of the partition; the block path gives the unstructured path's results bitwise, and the Numerics section of the log reports MUSCL's rows and block path (#1035).
