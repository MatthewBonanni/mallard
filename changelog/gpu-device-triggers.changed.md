- `[[triggers]]` are evaluated on the device at every step, from `t`, the step, `dt` and the monitors' rows: the device stops for the host only at a step a trigger fires at, so runs with triggers no longer stop the step queue at every step. Triggers that read the wall time or a probe's value still do (#730).
- `[statistics.convergence]` samples the rows of its signals from the step queue's records, and triggers on `convergence_<column>` values are evaluated on the device, which stops for the host only at the rows of the convergence's signals; the batch means of `[statistics] batch` are sampled on the device.
