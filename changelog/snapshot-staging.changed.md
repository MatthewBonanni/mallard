- Snapshots and restart files no longer stop the steps enqueued ahead of the GPU (`[run] step_queue`): the step graph computes the fields they hold and copies them into pinned host memory, and the host writes them while the GPU goes on. Files are the same bit for bit. Runs with `[sources]`, regions that move or follow the solution, fused chemistry, averaged spectra or phase averages still stop at their outputs. CPU builds keep a host copy of the output fields apart from the solver's arrays. (#906)
