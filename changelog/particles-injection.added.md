- `[[particles.injectors]]`: particles seeded during the run at points, over boundary zones (along their inward normal) or in filled regions, at a `rate` of parcels or a `mass_flow`, between `start` and `end`, with a velocity, a cone of directions, a `weight` and `temperature`, and diameters from size distributions (Rosin–Rammler, log-normal, Nukiyama–Tanasawa, uniform, tabulated; by number or mass). Counter-based random numbers make the injected particles bitwise the same on any rank count and with step graphs, restarts continue the injection exactly, the default `capacity` holds what the injectors emit over the run, and `mallard check --format json` reports each injector in `particle_injectors` (#759).
