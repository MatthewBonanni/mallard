- Synthetic turbulence on an `inlet_state` whose `u` depends on `t`: the fluctuations ride on the mean evaluated on the device at every stage, with the statistics of the fluctuations unchanged; `convection_velocity` defaults to the mean inflow speed at `t = 0`. A `reynolds_stress` of `t`, or a `u` of `t` together with `turbulence.profile`, is an error naming the key to change (#600).
