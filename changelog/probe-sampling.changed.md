- Probes no longer read samples outside the domain from the nearest cell. A `point` or `points` probe with a point outside the domain is now an input error that names the point and its distance to the nearest boundary zone; curves and resampled surfaces drop their samples outside the domain with a warning (`W-PROBE-OUTSIDE`, also in `mallard.jsonl`) and record the dropped samples as `clipped` in the probe file, and one entirely outside is an error. A point on a boundary face counts as inside (#673).
- Warnings issued during setup (deprecated keys, clipped probes, output formats) are now written to `mallard.jsonl` too, right after the run's `start` record (#673).
