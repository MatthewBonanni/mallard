- Regions repeat: `repeat = {count, translate}` or `{count, rotate, pivot}`, or a list of them for arrays of arrays, makes a region the union of its copies. Source expressions read the copy's index as `copy` (each copy its own term and, with `total = true`, its own total), and region probes with `copies = "each"` write one reductions file per copy. Disjoint copies measure their sum in closed form.
- Regions move: centers, sizes, transforms and implicit `f` may be expressions of `t`. Sources, sponges and filled-region probes follow them, with the cell fractions recomputed on the device at each stage (curved cells on their curved shapes) and totals of moving regions renormalized by device exact sums (NCCL across ranks inside CUDA graphs), so captured steps follow them without host work. Region probes write the region's measure in the domain at each write, and with `average = true` the time averages over the cells it sweeps.
- Regions that cross a translational or rotational periodic boundary wrap into the domain instead of being cut off, and keep their own measures.
