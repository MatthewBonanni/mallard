- The progress table of a run gives the units of its columns in a row under their names, keeps one unit per column (`wall/step` in ms, `throughput` in cells/s, `ETA` as h:mm:ss) so every column reads straight down, and opens with a note on what the columns are. Files written no longer interrupt it: its last column, `written`, counts them since the previous row (`2 vtu, 1 restart`); `mallard.jsonl` and the summary name them. Requests acted on and a divergence print a remark between the rows (`>> step 339, t = 0.061 s: stop: ...`) (#659, #675).
- The end-of-run Summary and Health sections are tables: wall time by phase with its share, throughput, bytes written and files by kind; the lowest density, pressure and temperature with their units, step and location; activity by rank, repairs and the conservation ledger (#659).
