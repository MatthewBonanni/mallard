- `viscous_gradients = "reconstruction"` reaches walls and symmetry planes: the wall shear stress and heat flux, and the viscous fluxes of the cells whose TENO stencils mirror cells across a wall, come from one-sided least-squares fits that take the wall's velocity and isothermal temperature at the wall's quadrature points instead of the mirror images, whose odd reflection gave first-order gradients there; symmetry faces take the cell's polynomial. The viscous residual next to walls of a manufactured solution with curved wall profiles now converges (TENO5: third order on triangles, second on quadrilaterals next to adiabatic walls) where least squares does not. The polynomial point data also holds the values of u and T, a third more memory in 3D (#1147).
