- The keyed 3D TENO setup computes cell moments only for the cells that fitted stencils read; the other cells of a stencil shape take their fitted cell's moments, as they take its tables (#1166). `mallard post derive` of the 64³ Taylor-Green vortex sets up about a fifth faster. With sector stencils (runs), the shared moments differ from each cell's own at round-off, so results change at round-off and TENO caches of earlier versions are recomputed.
