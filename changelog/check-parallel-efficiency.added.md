- `mallard check`'s cost estimate models parallel efficiency: the step time on N ranks is that of the largest part the run's partitioner makes, from its owned and halo cells, neighbors and halo on other nodes, by a model per device class (A100, CPUs) fitted to measured runs, and the throughput of one rank comes from the measurements for the device, dimension, reconstruction and physics when neither `--throughput` nor `--steps` gives it. The report gives wall time, compute hours and efficiency with their uncertainty, and a serial check adds a row per rank count; the JSON `cost` section gains the basis, the model and `scaling` (additive). New `--ranks-per-node`; `tools/perf/cost_model.py` regenerates the coefficients from `benchmarks/cost_model/` (#1440).
